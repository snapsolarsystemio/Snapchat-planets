Snapchat Planets Order and Meaning Explained (2026)

Confused about Mercury, Venus, Earth, or the other planets on Snapchat? This guide explains the Friend Solar System, what each planet means, how to check your position, and what to do if the feature does not appear.

Quick answer: Snapchat’s Friend Solar System is a Snapchat+ feature that uses eight planets to represent positions in a person’s Best Friends list. Mercury is first, Venus second, Earth third, Mars fourth, Jupiter fifth, Saturn sixth, Uranus seventh, and Neptune eighth.

What Is the Snapchat Friend Solar System?

Snapchat’s Friend Solar System is a feature for Snapchat+ subscribers. It represents friendship positions with planets. In this system, you are the Sun, while the planets represent people in your Best Friends list according to your interaction ranking.

Mercury represents the first position, while Neptune represents the eighth. The ranking is connected to how you interact on Snapchat, but Snapchat does not publish an exact formula or fixed interaction percentages for each planet.

How Does the Friend Solar System Work?

On a Friendship Profile, Snapchat+ subscribers may see a Best Friends or Friends badge with a gold outline. Tapping the badge can show the planet that represents your position in that person’s Friend Solar System.

  • Best Friends: Snapchat indicates that you are among each other’s top eight Best Friends.
  • Friends: You are among their top eight, but they are not necessarily among yours.

The feature may be off by default for new Snapchat+ subscribers. If you do not see it, check the Snapchat+ feature settings in your app.

How to Enable Snapchat Planets

  1. Open Snapchat and tap your profile or Bitmoji.
  2. Open the Snapchat+ membership area.
  3. Find the Solar System setting or feature management option.
  4. Turn the feature on if it is available to your account.
  5. Open a friend’s profile and look for the Best Friends or Friends badge.
Snapchat can update menus and feature availability. If the setting is missing, update the app and check Snapchat’s current support information for your account and region.

How to Check Your Snapchat Solar System

  1. Make sure you have access to Snapchat+.
  2. Open the profile of the friend you want to check.
  3. Look for the gold-outlined Best Friends or Friends badge.
  4. Tap the badge to view the planet shown for your position, if the feature is available.

You can only see the position Snapchat displays for your friendship. The feature is not a public view of every planet in another person’s complete Best Friends list.

How Does Snapchat Decide Your Planet Ranking?

Snapchat does not publicly explain every detail of the ranking formula. In general, Best Friends are based on how people interact on Snapchat. Activities that may matter include:

  • Snap activity: How often you send Snaps to each other.
  • Chat activity: How regularly you exchange messages.
  • Two-way interaction: Whether both people interact, rather than one person doing all the contacting.
  • Other activity: Snapchat may use its own systems to determine the order, and the exact weight of each signal is not public.

There is no confirmed public rule that a certain number of Snaps, a particular percentage, or a six-month streak guarantees a specific planet.

Tips for Building a Stronger Snapchat Friendship

  • Send Snaps when you genuinely want to share something.
  • Reply to your friend’s messages and stories naturally.
  • Keep conversations going when both of you want to chat.
  • Maintain a Snapstreak if it is fun for both of you, but remember that a streak alone does not guarantee a planet position.
  • Focus on real interaction instead of trying to game a ranking.

Common Myths About Snapchat Planets

  • Myth: Streaks alone decide your planet. There is no public confirmation that streak length alone controls planet position.
  • Myth: You can manually choose Mercury. Snapchat does not provide a standard control for manually assigning planets to friends.
  • Myth: Friends can see your entire Solar System. The feature shows a person their own position when available; it does not provide a public list of all your ranked friends.
  • Myth: Every planet has a fixed interaction percentage. Snapchat does not publish official percentage ranges for each planet.

Why Snapchat Planets Might Not Appear

  • You may need an active Snapchat+ subscription to access the feature.
  • The Solar System option may be turned off in Snapchat+ settings.
  • The other person may not be in the relevant Best Friends list.
  • Your Snapchat app may need an update.
  • The feature may not be available in the same way for every account or region.
  • A temporary app issue may be affecting the display. Try restarting the app or clearing its cache from Snapchat settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the order of Snapchat best friend planets?

The order is Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. They represent positions one through eight.

How many planets are in Snapchat’s Friend Solar System?

There are eight planets, corresponding to the top eight positions in the Best Friends list.

Can my friends see which planet they are in my Solar System?

If the feature is available and enabled, a friend may tap the relevant badge on your profile to see their own position. They cannot use it to view every friend’s position in your complete list.

Do I need Snapchat+ to use Snapchat Planets?

The Friend Solar System is a Snapchat+ feature. Availability and settings may change, so check the current options in your app.

Does a long Snapstreak guarantee Mercury?

No. Snapchat does not publicly confirm that streak length alone determines a planet position.

Why is my Snapchat Solar System turned off?

The feature may be disabled by default for some subscribers. Open your Snapchat+ settings and look for the Solar System option.
